## What This Work Involves

- **Voice matching** - posts that sound like the client, not like a marketing team
- **Technical depth** - content that holds up in front of engineers and CTOs
- **Point of view** - each post argues something, instead of restating common advice
- **Audience fit** - written for engineering leaders and technical decision-makers
